About Sebastian

One guy, every kind of ride.

Sebastian runs Pena Detailing Solutions out of Pinellas County, driving to clients across St. Petersburg, Tampa, Clearwater, and Pinellas Park. If it has a seat and a set of wheels, or floats, he's probably detailed one: sedans, trucks, mopeds, motorcycles, golf carts, and boats, all handled with the same close attention whether it's a daily driver or a weekend boat.

No shop, no drop-off. Just a fully equipped mobile setup that shows up on time and leaves things looking, and feeling, brand new. Every job is backed by a 200% happiness guarantee.
